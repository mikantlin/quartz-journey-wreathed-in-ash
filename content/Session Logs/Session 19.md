# What Actually Happened?
- The characters with 8 charisma are forced to try to deceive a hag. It goes as well as you'd expect (they get turned into frogs). During this time Jaswyn discovered that the stone things outside the windmill are sealing something, and that they're related to the ancient cities of paradise.
- Martyn does his best to find the book while Victor kidnaps the children, but we run out of time. The rest of the party barges in to keep frog Dirge and frog Helwyn from getting thrown into a boiling soup pot.
- Jaswyn calls forth the sun. It literally melts a hag from full to zero. Thus the mission is still a partial success and we get our hands on a heartstone.
- Two hags escape, but we know their true names now. Helwyn and Jaswyn used 3 days to learn the binding ritual that the baroness was supposed to use, so we'll have options when they do strike. Martyn uses his to get half proficiency in religion.
- While they're doing that, Dirge goes on a 3-day trip with Father Lucien and gets promoted. His direct superior is not thrilled that the new chapter master is a vampire (since they're one of the more militant sects in the church), but begrudgingly accepts it.
- By the way @Simeon (Arran) apparently your grimoire infinitus has now gone from Dormant to Awakened too.
- Ritual to bring Stella (that's her name right?) back to her body takes place. We have to defend her soul while it happens, but the goddamn spirits are smart and ignore us, attacking her directly. Poor girl very nearly dies.
- We succeed. Absolutely nothing else of consequence happened. No very stupid and impulsive decisions were made, trust me.
- Incidentally, we learn that the ghost boy Eremis (spell check?) is Van Richten's dead son, along with some other important plot revelations.
