# What Actually Happened?
## Beat 1
- Helwyn and Dirge go on the charm offensive (obviously) to the Old Bonegrinder windmill. We're asked what we want by a lady introducing herself as Marge. Dirge introduces his plantation-owner alter ego Clayton Walker-Creek, spilling out the most *show-stopping* accent straight from the Deep South. Who's the devil with him? That's, uh, Dirge. Just Dirge. Great start, people!
- Dirge (sorry, *Clayton*) wants to present to Marge a potential business venture between us three, helping the ~~hag~~distinguished madam to franchise her dream pastry business across the whole of Alvastr, maybe even the world! Marge is dubious, but she leads us inside to discuss further and show us the operations (nice, that's our foot in the door). 
- Meanwhile, our resident sneak captain/Professional Old Man Martyn is made invisible by Victor and sneaks himself up and in through the top of the windmill. He finds Marge's daughters Bella and Ophelia inside, but manages to avoid their attention by standing still in corners and letting radiant explosions take care of the rest (don't worry, I'm getting there). Martyn searches the attic (nothing), third floor (probably nothing) and second floor (nothing), but turns up nothing like the Big Book of Finding Hags' Names we're looking for.
- Also meanwhile, Jaswyn is inspecting the obelisk she and Arran had spotted, although Arran disappears into the mists as they head towards (give the guy a break, he IS blind after all). Something's moving in the fog near the obelisk. Maybe Arran? The mists take a chunk out of Jaswyn before darting out of sight. Probably not Arran.
- The mist-shark takes another bite out of our good healer, leading Jaswyn to make the correct call of "fuck this shit" and fire off what is basically *the Sun*, destroying whatever bit her and exploding a good radius of the mists around the obelisk. Clearly trained at the Ismark School of Subtle Arcana, that one.
## Beat 2
- Marge hears and sees Jaswyn's light show and is immediately on the alert, heading straight out. Not-Dirge and Clayton tag along as they make their way to the obelisk to find a pretty good area of scorched earth around the place (luckily, Jaswyn has the sense to stay out of sight). Clayton shrugs; this is classic kid stuff, right? You know kids! He remembers a kid... Not-Dirge argues about that kid, no not the ginger one, the other one... 
- Marge ain't buying what we're selling (absolutely fair, what we're selling is awful). She tells us to leave and starts to head back to the windmill. In last-ditch attempt to keep her talking, we *hag*gle (HAH) for some dream pastries for the road. She'll sell us one to share (which, if you know Dirge, is nowhere near enough food.) We head back into the windmill at her entirely-normal-and-not-at-all-suspicious behest.
- Jaswyn creeps out and checks out the obelisk again. They have carvings related to the Ancient Cities of Paradise (whatever that is).  There's also some amber-like crystals around its base, and a weird black ooze on its surface, which Jaswyn scientifically confirms is Bad Stuff by touching it and burning her fingerprints off. She goes back to see Lady Wachter to ask about them, who says that other obelisks around the country seem to be designed to contain something - maybe Strahd? None of them are oozy-black though, so that's weird. She does remember some of the roads having teeth though, so there's- wait, *teeth*? 
## Beat 3
- Back in the mill, Marge insists on the two charmers taking a seat at the kitchen table as one of her kids does something to the door, AKA locking it magically. They begin to transform into horrific hag creatures (shocking, I know!) as they rip their skins off. Clayton's a southern boy and knows when it's rude to outstay someone's hospitality, so Helwyn and Dirge get up to leave, but the hags hit 'em with the old Polymorph, turning them into frogs. Hmm, not a great start to this fight.
- Lily and Jaswyn get wind of this and come charging to our rescue! Oh, and if you're wondering where Victor is, he is very methodically Misty Stepping one small child at a time out of there. He may be slow, but dammit, he is EFFECTIVE. Lily bursts down the mill door and the cavalry come on in.
- Martyn's still see-through, so he comes down and decides this is absolutely the best time to start patting down hags for books and searching the place (don't mind the fight or anything, Martyn). Jaswyn, still riding that sunlight cannon high from earlier, casts Ray of Holy-Shit-My-Eyes-I-Can't-See straight at Bella and completely obliterates her with radiant power.
- Marge and Ophelia take the hint and scarper, leaving threats behind as they vanish into the Ethereal. Martyn works out that the book MUST be on the third floor and goes back for a second look-around. There it is! Final score: the Big Book of Finding Hags' Names, a Heartstone, the smoking ashes of one hag (AKA Bella Sunbane) and two other hags (AKA Morgantha Stormreaver and Ophelia Wiggleworm) vowing to come gunning for us. Sounds about right for our party.
## Beat 4
# TL;DR
- The characters with 8 charisma are forced to try to deceive a hag. It goes as well as you'd expect (they get turned into frogs). During this time Jaswyn discovered that the stone things outside the windmill are sealing something, and that they're related to the ancient cities of paradise.
- Martyn does his best to find the book while Victor kidnaps the children, but we run out of time. The rest of the party barges in to keep frog Dirge and frog Helwyn from getting thrown into a boiling soup pot.
- Jaswyn calls forth the sun. It literally melts a hag from full to zero. Thus the mission is still a partial success and we get our hands on a heartstone.
- Two hags escape, but we know their true names now. Helwyn and Jaswyn used 3 days to learn the binding ritual that the baroness was supposed to use, so we'll have options when they do strike. Martyn uses his to get half proficiency in religion.
- While they're doing that, Dirge goes on a 3-day trip with Father Lucien and gets promoted. His direct superior is not thrilled that the new chapter master is a vampire (since they're one of the more militant sects in the church), but begrudgingly accepts it.
- By the way @Simeon (Arran) apparently your grimoire infinitus has now gone from Dormant to Awakened too.
- Ritual to bring Stella (that's her name right?) back to her body takes place. We have to defend her soul while it happens, but the goddamn spirits are smart and ignore us, attacking her directly. Poor girl very nearly dies.
- We succeed. Absolutely nothing else of consequence happened. No very stupid and impulsive decisions were made, trust me.
- Incidentally, we learn that the ghost boy Eramus (spell check?) is Van Richten's dead son, along with some other important plot revelations.
