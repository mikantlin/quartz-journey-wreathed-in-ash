# What Actually Happened?
## No Getting Away
- The fight with an incredibly pissed-off [[Rahadin]] continues. [[Dirge]] manages to turn 400 of the undead that are attacking the town and us, leaving only... \*does the maths\* 1,600 angry elven spirits to deal with. Easy! [[Martyn]] falls into an old-man nap (AKA magically-induced stress-related coma). Rahadin goes hand-to-hand with [[Doru]] and [[Helwyn]], who gains multiple stab wounds (this is why you don't bring fists to a sword fight) and the spirits paralyze [[Arran]] with their screams, but we manage to start whittling him down
- On the ropes now, Rahadin books it to the exit and throws up a wall behind him, blocking us from chasing him down. Luckily, a healer in the town sees Rahadin and recognises him as a threat, hitting him with an acid arrow. It should kill him, but Rahadin's wearing an 'in case of emergency' amulet that keeps him standing
- Dirge misty steps through the barrier and sees the healer's attack. He casts Blight on Rahadin and disintegrates him. The spirits fade away and the wall blocking the rest of the party crumbles. We've finally put down one of [[Strahd]]'s people for good!
## A Lesson in Mercy
- The healer introduces herself as [[Jaswyn]] and that she was hired by [[Ismark the Lesser|Ismark]] to help support the town against the siege. Ismark requests that she joins him and the party on the road to [[Knafgata]] to help provide medical support. Arran grabs Rahadin's mini-scimitars so that he can finally do some more up-close-and-personal damage if he needs to (Helwyn mental note: hide Arran's spellbook so we get that going quicker)
- Helwyn spots amongst the [[Order of Falling Ash|Order]]'s people a man from her past: [[Lukas]], one of the mercenaries who helped kill [[Yrsa]]. Anger and vengeance fills her as she goes after him. Lukas recognises her and desperately tries to explain himself, but Helwyn ignores him, demanding to know where she can find the other men from that night. The rest of the order make their excuses and move away, and the party keeping an eye on things (man, this party is WAY too comfortable with painful interrogations)
- Dirge casts Zone of Truth to help, while Helwyn casts Zone of Stabbing Someone in the Leg. Shockingly effective! Lukas reveals where he last heard about the others: [[Marcus]], their leader, is currently hiding out as a merchant in [[Endkleistra]]; another is masquerading as a priest in [[Knafgata]]; and the final one is [[Usi-Ziyad|Usi]]'s inside man in Strahd's inner circle. Guess she's not getting to HIM anytime soon, especially if Strahd finds out about a double-agent hanging around his pad
- Helwyn agrees to let Lukas live, but shows (the most FUCKED-UP) mercy to him: She drops him to the ground and slices up his sciatic nerve, inflicting massive amounts of pain upon him. Dirge casts Silence to mask the man's screaming from the horrified townsfolk. Ismark says to Helwyn that she gave the man no mercy, and that she should have decapitated him (an honourable mercy). She responds by telling him to stick his judgment until he comes face-to-face with his own family's killer, and walks away from the group
## Loose Ends
- [[Usi-Ziyad|Usi]] shows up and discusses with the group their agreement, taking a pause to casually roast Lukas to death with a firebolt (ironically, that's fucking cold). He checks that [[Ludmilla]] is still alive and says that his business with the party is now concluded, so we're on our own from now on (oh no...) As Usi heads away, Ismark suggests killing him to help ensure that his cult is stopped and him being the cause of Rahadin's death is made more likely. The party refuse, stating that without Usi and the Order's help they would not have been able to bring Rahadin down. See, we DO have (some) morals! Dirge also points out that without the Sunburst, we have no real guarantee of killing Usi before he could escape through mirror shenanigans. It's a risky plan, even for us
- Arran takes this time to talk to some Order members, asking how they are okay with Usi leaving them during the fight. They moralise that Usi was looking after his family, a loyal thing to do. They also argue about the politics of the situation, the Order guys claiming that their group are focused on returning the world to its natural state through fire. Arran's dubious about this, thinking them mindless followers
- Helwyn throws up in an alleyway once out of sight from the group, struggling to process what she's done. After an hour or so of moping there, she eventually drags herself to [[Canemere]]'s workshop, determined to fix one broken part at least. She gets stupidly lucky and finds a magic eye that can see invisibility. As the others show up, she also finds 3 [Supreme Potions of Healing](https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/5135-potion-of-healing-supreme), a ring of lightning resistance (that goes to [[M0-TH3R|Mother]] to avoid any short-circuit situations) and a [Corpse Slayer Sling](https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/wondrous-items:corpse-slayer), which Dirge promptly smacks himself in the face with
## On the Road Again
- We take a breather, then head off towards [[Knafgata]] to find [[Madame Eva]]. Once we're on Svalich Road and heading through the [[Svalich Woods|woods]], we're stopped by the guardian of the forest, [[Ferret]]. She asks us if we want to buy her [[Ferret's Guide to the Svalich Woods|book]] (of course we do, have you seen how fucking CUTE that thing is?)
- We read about the adorable, absolutely-not-terrifying creatures and objects dwelling in the forest for a while, and ask her about her living here. She mentions that she doesn't know where her parents are and that [[Nice Uncle]] looks after her. She is also a follower of the [[the Lady of the Mists]] who Doru tells us 'helps people find where they wish to be needed'.
- Ferret tells us Nice Uncle can help us get to Madame Eva (an absolute NOPE for Helwyn's vote), but we politely decline the offer. Mother uses her divine sense and determines that Ferret is Celestial, which is probably how she's survived so long in these woods
- Wanting to give Ferret the [[Shtriga Nonna's Pot of Plenty|cauldron]] we left in Endkleistra, Mother's surprised to find it's now on her person! Must be because she was thinking so hard about it. Helwyn thinks intensely about the Chop Shop and the Bobs, and now they're here too! Freaking out like crazy of course, but here and alive. It's an emotional moment for Helwyn to see her ~~slaves~~ apprentices again
- Eventually, we ask Ferret about [[Rudolph Van Richten|Van Richten]]'s stash. She knows right where we can find it! What are the chances? (She's got his book too, but won't hand it over. Eh, we've got Doru who's a VR nerd, we'll be fine.) Once she takes us to the cache, it's surrounded by five [[Ferret's Guide to the Svalich Woods#The Friendly One|Friendly Ones]], watching it closely. Ferret reckons Mother has the best chance of getting it, as they tend to leave non-humans alone
- Mother manages to retrieve the cache and the Friendly Ones wander off now we've stolen their entertainment. Inside, we find 20 silvered bolts, a light crossbow (goes to Arran), 2 healer's kits (which will probably be for the Wyns) and 2 holy waters (I assume Dirge is knocking this back constantly)
## In the Cards
- We bid farewell to Ferret and head off to [[Freyja's Tear]] to find Madame Eva. On the way, a black carriage pulls up heading the opposite direction and [[Anastrasya]] steps out. Fuck... She's just coming back from Madame Eva where she requested some 'information', and informs us that her husband is royally pissed off. Comments are made around Rahadin's death which we (incredibly convincingly) feign ignorance around being involved in, and she warns us that the perpetrator will be made an example of by Strahd. After seeing Nomduhr, I'm assuming it'll be incredibly proportional to the crime. She threatens us with [[Volenta]], before claiming that she'll kill us the next time she sees us. Great chat as always, Anastrasya!
- We eventually reach Madame Eva at Freyja's Tear. She asks Doru to step into the blood waters to begin the process of breaking his ties with Strahd. While this is happening, she refuses to share what Anastrasya's business with her was, but asks us to pull cards for our own [[Madame Eva's Reading|reading]]. Guess we'll try and interpret the cards as best we can as, like Eva said, our fate is only known to us...
# TL;DR
- We managed to kill Rahadin, thanks to the help of a new ally and party member, Jaswyn. Helwyn tortured a mercenary who was involved in the death of her mentor
- We stumbled upon a young girl living in the Svalich Woods who helped us find Van Richten's stash, as well as telling us all about the weird and wonderful things in the woods. We managed to somehow wish the Chop Shop and the Bobs to the Woods
- We met with Anastraya on the way to Madame Eva. She warns us that her husband is not happy with us and that she would kill us next time we met her. Madame Eva gave us a tarot reading that could help guide us in our quest to bring down Strahd
