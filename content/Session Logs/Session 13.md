# What Actually Happened?
## Bird-Brained
- We finish up with [[Madame Eva]], who (kinda) politely asks us to leave. We check how [[Doru]] is doing, who says that he no longer has [[Strahd]]'s voice whispering in his mind. He also asks if he can eat any enemies that we take down.. Eh... not thrilled about it, but if it keeps our necks bite-free, go for it! On our way to [[Knafgata]], our path is interrupted by a familiar raven with blue-tipped wings. Only thing new about it is the dozen or so silver bolts sticking out of its chest (Ouch). [[The Bobs]] get to work helping recover the bird in the back of the [[Helwyn's Chop Shop|Chop Shop]], but it's quickly followed by scarecrow-like constructs that go on the offensive.
- We manage to dispatch them without too much of an issue, and [[Arran]] tries to use his vast academic knowledge to recall what these things are and who sent them. Good news: he scores a Nat 20! Earns himself a vision of a hag's house on chicken legs, which probably means one of the Big Baba Three. Bad news? The hag in question doesn't like psychic intruders and curses him with "a mind that dims in battle until the sun has set forever". Uh... yay?
- The raven is sentient, communicating with us through snippets of sound. It conveys that more stick monsters are coming, so it jumps onto [[M0-TH3R|Mother]]'s arm and we book it into the fog. We come out of the fog onto the narrow and precarious road of a mountain pass, which is strange given that we should be miles away from any mountain. Hopefully there's no wolves around!
## There's Wolves Around
- [[Helwyn]] tracks *something* following them in the mists, and eventually the Chop Shop is surrounded by wolves. Rabid wolves actually, so that's fun! One manages to drag Arran off the clifftop down to the forest floor 50 feet below. He tries and fails to catch a branch on the way down, but luckily, another wolf breaks his fall! Wait, more wolves? Shit.
- [[Jaswyn]] to the rescue! She heroically takes a shot at the wolves below, but Nat 1s it, so Arran instead catches a stray Firebolt to the back on his way down. It doesn't count as friendly fire if you Healing Word them immediately afterwards, right?
- Let's try this again... [[Martyn]] to the rescue! He heroically jumps off the cliff after Arran, Misty Stepping to the base. His Chaos Magic triggers and he rolls whatever number makes a wolf judge you for being a terrible husband who murdered his wife (I'm guessing, like, 6?) He drops to the floor and immediately starts sobbing like a baby.
- Sigh... Mother to the rescue! They slide down towards the mountain's edge on their shield, heroically inventing snowboarding as well as attempting to save Arran (If you're keeping count, that's over half of the party who have now jumped off this cliff.) The wolves are dealt with eventually, but Helwyn spots two larger werewolves watching from afar, clearly masterminding the whole thing. Wonder who they are?
## Troll Bridge
- Helwyn, Jaswyn and the Bobs follow the road down on the Chop Shop and regroup with the others. We continue on our way and eventually reach a bridge guarded by a lone undead knight. He asks us if we are followers of Strahd, then says we can carry on when we say we're not (great guarding work). Doru recognises him as one of the soldiers who joined him in the rebellion, and hides in case the guy blames Doru for his death (which is kinda fair.)
- We ask the knight how he determines who is good or bad, which basically boils down to vibe checks. He introduces himself as a Knight of the [[Order of the Palladium Dragon]] (AKA [[Thamak]]), tasked with defending the world against evil. Looks like specifically anything below 'medium evil' for this guy, as we find out after lengthy moral discussions. We also find out Arran's the most evil of the party because he's *mean*, so Helwyn feels much better about the torturing now!
- The knight tells us that he was part of an attack of [[Strahd's castle]]. He and his Order went against the orders of their lord, [[Sir Vladimir Horngaard]], to not attack Strahd and ended up dying. Now he's cursed by Horngaard to defend this specific bridge specifically against Strahd and specifically on horseback. Fingers crossed buddy! He also says that we should meet [[Lord Godfrey]] in the [[Shadowlow Mountains]], who is "a good man" that can help us (not that "good" is a particularly hard standard to hit for this guy), and that we should avoid the big werewolves (great advice!) before letting us continue on to Knafgata.
## Food? In THIS economy?
- We reach Knafgata to find masses of people outside the village, emaciated and suffering (not a good look for their tourism ads). [[Ismark the Lesser|Ismark]] kills one of our spare horses to start feeding them, while Jaswyn and Helwyn do their usual doctorly duties, like stopping a potential plague from spreading. Arran talks to the guards on the wall, who asks to see his teeth. Turns out they're keeping an eye out for vampires (so Doru stays out of sight) and are keeping out the people for 'disease' and 'malicious unhappiness' by order of [[Baron Vargas Vallakovich]]. Sounds like a great guy!
- Eventually convinced that we're not biters, the guard says that it's one gold each(!) to get in, and all silver has to be handed over. [[Dirge]] makes a counter-offer of some holy water, which gets him in but no-one else. Dammit. Eventually, Mother coughs up the dough for them to let us in, but we stash a majority of our silver in the wagon just in case leaving it outside with the Bobs and Doru (hopefully he doesn't eat anyone).
## The Scenic Route
- The guards lead us in towards the [[Knafgata#Blue Water Inn]], but we ask if we can see [[Father Lucien]] at the church first. The guard objects as the father's last communion is happening today, but we eventually win him over by being fucking annoying about it. We head to the church and see Father Lucien come out. We discuss with him around the people outside, but he believes that the Baron is doing the right thing. He is also working on a plan to stop Strahd for the good of the world, building a machine that will siphon Strahd's divinity from him and leave him weak and unprotected. Definitely not an insane plan!
- Ismark says he will stay in the church with Lucien and keep an eye on things, as we head over to the Inn. As we get there, the raven thanks Mother for the lift (sorta) and flies up to a group of ravens on the roof. Stepping inside, we find things are pretty quiet, except for the feather-hatted bard who's talking (A LOT.)
- The innkeeper, [[Erwin]], welcomes us and lets us know how crazy expensive everything is. Tends to happen when you commandeer all silvers in the place, I guess. He does mention that there is a senior discount for [[Martyn]] (YAAAAAY) but not on alcohol (BOOOOOO).
## Long Story Long
- [[Doctor Arthur Sedwick|Rictavio]] introduces himself, buys us three drinks each, and shares a long, *looooong* story about a vampire who lost his hair (those drinks come in *very* handy). After the story, he shares that he got to town this morning and doesn't remember where he was before. He asks for an interesting story from ourselves, which is difficult to do because BY THE GODS are our stories depressing (Jaswyn has a great one about a syphilis-delivering crossbow bolt, but otherwise we're running on empty).
- We ask him to maybe share his entertaining stories with the crowd outside (given that they need curing of that gosh-dang annoying unhappiness stuff they've been spreading around). He eventually agrees and very kindly gives his suite for the night, as he'll be slumming it with the folks outside.
## Sharing is Caring
- Heading up to Rictavio's room, Helwyn checks the place for any clue as to who the fuck this guy really is, but comes up with nothing. They talk about the Father's plans and what kind of machine he might mean. Dirge drunkenly explains how it might work by instilling divinity into a clock which, well, clarifies nothing really. He also keeps prestidigitating us (the man HATES germs, that's for sure).
- Arran asks Helwyn about what happened in [[Nomduhr]] and she shares out of the goodness of her heart and just being hammered. The guy she tortured was one of the mercenaries that helped kill her mentor, [[Yrsa]], and she is on a path of revenge. She also mentions the fact that one of the mercenaries is right here in Knafgata posing as a priest, but that she understands if no-one wants to help her with this. The party is oddly okay with the torture/murder part though (a therapist would probably have a field day with the lot of them).
## Hats Off to Him
- We ask what the deal is with the Baron and the people outside the gate, and he informs us that the Baron holds festivals every week in an attempt to keep Strahd at bay (presumably Strahd hates confetti?) Erwin also mentions that the Baron (or, more accurately, his right-hand man [[Izek]]) is quick to put any detractors to death, hanging people a couple of times a day. Yikes.
- As we're discussing the Baron, Rictavio drops by to pick up some supplies, and we press him about who exactly he is. Again. Yes, we're that stubborn. He talks round and round, and Arran points out the fact that 'Rictavio' and '[[Rudolph Van Richten|Van Richten]]' have some of the same letters. COINCIDENCE?
- I mean, probably. Buuuuut we ALSO remember that Doru met Van Richten while he wore a hat of disguise so he didn't look like himself. We try to get Rictavio's hat (seriously, it gets close to assault), before he finally asks us to come along to the stables where his horse is. There he reveals that he IS IN FACT... [[Doctor Arthur Sedwick]], a mentee of Van Richten and a fellow member of the [[Keepers of the Feather]] (whatever that is).
- Sedwick is looking for Van Richten, and he might just need our help to do it. Between that and a hasty revolution that we're already planning, it looks like we might be sticking around for a while, people...
# TL;DR
- The party head for Knafgata, attacked on the way by a hag's minions and wolves. Crossing a bridge guarded by an unusually judgemental undead knight and eventually reaching the town, they find a large amount of its people starved and shut outside, by order of Baron Vargas Vallakovich
- Once the party finally manage to buy our way in, they track down Father Lucien to find out that he is building some sort of machine to strip Strahd of his divinity and stop his oppression of Endkleistra. A suspicious activity, given the help he could be offering to the people of his own town instead
- Taking lodging at the local inn, the party encounters Rictavio, a strange and feather-hatted entertainer who shares stories and asks for theirs as well, buying them a few drinks in the process. They decided to trade for him using his music to cheer up the outside masses and he agrees, giving the party his suite for the night. The party's conversation turns to Helwyn's sudden torture session in Nomduhr and how one of her targets are in Knafgata too
- In the morning, the party speaks to the innkeeper about the Baron and how terrible the town is. The party presses Rictavio about his background and identity, and eventually convinces him to trust in them, taking his hat of disguise off to reveal... Arthur Sedwick, a follower of Van Richten who's looking for his mentor
