# What Actually Happened?
## Rough Notes
- While Arran, Helwyn, Dirge and Martyn are off investigating for Father Lucien, Mother has been helping out in the Blue Water Inn's kitchen, making meals for the customers
- Urwin mentions to her that a scruffy-looking man in a brown cloak had brought a letter addressed to her. Inside the letter is a dinner invitation to the party from one Lady Fiona Wachter of Wachterhaus
- An exhausted and fairly beaten-up Martyn comes storming into the inn with some surprisingly good-quality wood planks and makes his way to the suite, Mother saluting him as he passes
- Mother heads out into the town to try and locate the rest of the party. She bumps into Izek, who says he's been looking for the "blonde twink boy" AKA Ismark. When she asks why, Izek says that he's really into Ismark and would like to see him. Mother thinks nothing of this for some reason, nor his monstrous arm, so she offers to take him to the church since that's where Ismark was last
- Once they get to the church, Ismark is visibly surprised that Mother has brought Izek to find him, slowly reaching for his sword. She reads the tension as a relationship blossoming rather than an immediate threat turning up, so she heads out to give the two privacy and find us
- Meanwhile, Father Lucien has offered to provide us a blessing from the church for the work we did in helping him, but he has to perform the rites while consecrating the bones of St. Andral. Dirge thinks that Father Lucien has no idea if this'll work, and he's right: it's the first time Lucien has done it, so we're guinea pigs 
- About 15 minutes away from the church, the party (minus Martyn who's sleeping off the fight and Jaswyn who is... somewhere?) finally come back together. Mother tells us about how she found a man her height who wanted to find Ismark. Naturally, we find that incredibly worrying. It gets more worrying when she describes his giant arm
- Quickly rushing back to the church, we find that everything is smashed to pieces. Clearly a fight happened here, but there's no bodies. We do however find some blood that Mother analyses (ew) and determines is not Ismark's. We determine that Ismark tried to slice up Izek, but Izek never needed or chose to use a weapon. Then again, his arm is pretty much all the weapon he probably needs
- Dirge gets his hound/mount Rosemary to sniff the blood and see if she can find out where the owner's gone. She picks up the scent and we follow it, eventually turning up outside the Baron's house. On the way, Mother mentions about the invitation that we received. Some of us are skeptical about being invited to another dinner hosted by an unknown person, but Arran *really* wants a fancy dinner. He asks Helwyn not to ruin this one - Hey! She doesn't know how fancy dinners are meant to work, but she knows an asshole lord when she sees one. Arran tells her to play the game more, but she doesn't make any promises
- While we're talking, a man comments on how interesting our party is. He's been standing there for 20 minutes listening to us. He asks if we hate Strahd, to which we reply "Who's asking?" He introduces himself as Vasili Von Holtz, a nobleman from up north, and says that he hates vampires and has killed three vampire nobles so far
- He asks if we know who killed Volenta. Obviously not us haha! We talk around it a little and after some beating around the bush, Dirge reckoning he's trustworthy, we take the conversation into a secluded alleyway and he opens the bag to reveal three severed heads - disgusting. Dirge offers to prestidigitate them to get rid of the smell, but Vasili says he likes it - more disgusting
- He tells that he saw us kill Volenta, but wanted to make sure that we didn't just spill the beans to just anybody, as we wouldn't be worth working with against Strahd. He says that he can help with getting Ismark back, as he's been watching the Baron's house for about a week now. He knows how to get Izek's room on the top floor via the Baron's kid's room. Vasili's been watching him, but not in a creepy way: The kid has arcane powers and is trying to become a lich.
- Vasili: inside are 12 guards, 3 staff, wife and kid
- Vasili knocks on door and causes distraction while we grab Ismark and get out
- Dirge uses mage hand to open the kid's window from the inside and we climb in
- Kid has storybooks written by Strahd, but the have the Alarm spell on them
- Mother gives Helwyn her amulet of disguise so helwyn can pretend to be a maid and sneak to Izek's room
- Helwyn can't think of anyone, so she becomes a female version of Arran dressed as a maid
- The headmaid is at the other end of the hallway and she spots Helwyn, asking who she is. Helwyn bullshits about another headmaid hiring her, acting all confused. The woman is confused and goes to head downstairs, so Helwyn knocks her out, dragging her back to the party
- Helwyn picks the lock on the door to Izek's room and peaks inside. Izek has Ismark gagged and tied and they're curled up in bed with Izek wrapped around Ismark
- We decide dirge will long-cast silence around the two, Mother will go in and get Ismark out, Arran will ready phantasmal killer to fuck with Izek if he wakes up and Helwyn will watch for other staff
- As Mother tries to cut Ismark free, sleeping Izek places his hand on her arm. She tries to move it away without him noticing, but he wakes up
- Arran fires phantasmal killer, which works, but Izek gets advantage on attacks when afraid
- Izek creates a block of fire where arran and dirge are. Arran gets inside and takes on Izek, but Izek grabs him and sends him through the walls and outside the house. Arran misty steps back into the house before he hits the ground
- Helwyn goes into the room and manages to drag Ismark out via the hole Arran made
- Mother gets slammed through the floor and ceiling before being thrown into a corner
- Helwyn attacks Izek at the shoulder where his arm joins, damaging it and enfuriating Izek
- Helwyn gets grabbed and thrown back through the wall on the other side of the room, landing in the kid's room
- Baron and Vasili come upstairs, with Baron screaming for Izek to kill them. Vasili checks if complications should be dealt with, Dirge says yep, Vasili stabs the Baron through the chest and disappears
- Arran cuts Ismark's arms free and he ungags himself. He says that his sword was handed off to someone on the way to the Baron's house
- They jump out of the hole down to the ground
- Helwyn jumps out of the kid's window, but Izek throws his axe into her shoulder. She flies down to the ground
- Izek follows the rest of the party out and attacks them. Doru turns up as he has seen the pillar of smoke from outside the town. We say he can eat Izek, so Doru bites him and they teleport 60 feet up into the air
- Izek grabs Doru and sends him slamming through the house to the other side
- Helwyn comes around the house and flies up, getting on attack in as Izek comes down and hits the ground
- Izek explodes outwards with a fire attack damaging everyone. Helwyn almost dies and falls out of the air, but she's caught by Mother
- Izek goes for further fire explosions, taking down Dirge, Mother and Izek. Arran is the only one still standing, and he manages to fire Magic Missiles point blank into Izek's arm join, killing him
- Arran manages to stabilise Mother and Dirge, but Helwyn dies from her wounds. Doru comes back and Arran asks him to try and bring back Dirge, but the only way he can do that is by making Dirge undead. He does it though
- Arran goes to Helwyn and grabs the key that Glasya gave her. He invokes Glasya's name, telling her that if she doesn't want it going to Strahd, she better help Helwyn come back right now
