# What Actually Happened?
## Mother the Matchmaker
- While Arran, Helwyn, Dirge and Martyn are off investigating for Father Lucien, Mother has been working in the Blue Water Inn's kitchen for... some reason. Urwin mentions to her that a scruffy-looking man in a brown cloak had brought a letter addressed to her. Inside the letter is a dinner invitation to the party from one Lady Fiona Wachter of Wachterhaus. More out-of-the-blue dinners with people we don't know - gotta go better than last time, right?
- An exhausted and fairly beaten-up Martyn comes storming into the inn with some surprisingly good-quality wood planks and makes his way to the suite, clearly in a bad mood. Mother salutes him as he passes (wise choice, probably best not getting caught in THAT storm right now).
- Mother heads out into the town to try and locate the rest of the party. She bumps into Izek, who says he's been looking for the "blonde twink boy" AKA Ismark. When she asks why, Izek says that he's really into Ismark and would like to see him. Mother thinks nothing of why a giant man with a monstrous arm might be looking for an enemy of Strahd, so of course she offers to take him to the church to find Ismark.
- Once they get to the church, Ismark is visibly surprised that Mother has brought Izek to find him, slowly reaching for his sword. She reads the tension as a relationship blossoming rather than a shitshow about to kick off, so she heads out to give the two privacy and find the rest of the party.
## Red Flags
- Speaking of shitshows: After the aftermath of the coffin shop, Father Lucien has offered to provide us a blessing from the church for the work we did in helping him, but he has to perform the rites while consecrating the bones of St. Andral. Dirge thinks that Father Lucien has no idea if this'll work, and he's right: it's the first time Lucien has done it, so we're guinea pigs for his little holy experiment. Fun! 
- When we get 15 minutes away from the church, the party (minus Martyn who's sleeping off the fight and Jaswyn who is... somewhere?) finally come back together. Mother tells us about how she found a man who wanted to find Ismark (uh oh.) He was also taller than her and had a giant arm (UH OH).
- Quickly rushing back to the church, we find that everything is smashed to pieces. Clearly a fight happened here, but there's no bodies. We do however find some blood that Mother analyses by tasting it (ew) and determines that it's not Ismark's blood. Ismark must have tried to slice up Izek, but Izek probably never needed or chose to draw a weapon. Then again, that battering ram he calls an arm is pretty much all the weapon he probably needs.
## Making Headway
- Dirge gets his hound/mount Rosemary to sniff the blood and see if she can find out where the owner's gone. She picks up the scent and we follow it, eventually turning up outside the Baron's house. On the way, Mother remembers about the invitation that we received. Some of us are sceptical about being invited to another dinner hosted by an unknown person, but Arran *really* wants a fancy dinner. He asks Helwyn not to ruin this one (Hey, she might not know how fancy dinners are meant to work, but she knows an asshole lord when she sees one). Arran tells her to play the game more (as if).
- While we're talking, a man comments on how interesting our party is. He's been standing there for 20 minutes listening to us. He asks if we hate Strahd, to which we reply "Who's asking?" He introduces himself as Vasili Von Holtz, a nobleman from up north, and says that he hates vampires and has killed three vampire nobles so far - impressive! He asks if we know who killed Volenta. Obviously not us, haha, what?! We talk around it a little and after some beating around the bush (and Dirge reckoning he's trustworthy), we take the conversation to a secluded alleyway.
- Vasili opens the bag to reveal three severed vampire heads (disgusting) as proof. Dirge offers to prestidigitate them to get rid of the smell, but Vasili says he likes it (even more disgusting). Vasili tells that he saw us kill Volenta, but wanted to make sure that we didn't just spill the beans to just anybody, as we wouldn't be worth working with against Strahd. He says that he can help with getting Ismark back, as he's been watching the Baron's house for about a week now. 
## Operation: Don't Fuck This Up
- According to Vasili, inside the Baron's house are 12 guards, 3 staff, a wife and and a creepy kid aspiring to be a lich. Here's his plan: Vasili knocks on the door and causes distraction while we get into the top floor via the Baron's kid's room. Vasili's been watching him (not in a creepy way - the kid has arcane powers and is trying to become a lich). Izek's room is the one next door, so we get in there, grab Ismark and get out. Easy peasy!
- Dirge uses Mage Hand to open the kid's bedroom window from the inside and we climb in. The bookshelf has children's storybooks written by Strahd (everyone needs a hobby I guess), but the have the Alarm spell on them so we have no way to see what unhinged shit he's written in them.
- Mother gives Helwyn her amulet of disguise so helwyn can pretend to be a maid and sneak to Izek's room without drawing attention. Helwyn goes blank of any person ever, so in a moment of desperation she becomes a female version of Arran dressed as a maid (no further questions).
- Helwyn subtly makes her way along towards Izek's room, but the maid at the end of the hallway spots her, asking who she is. Helwyn bullshits about being hired by the headmaid. Turns out that's who she's talking to. Whoops. Helwyn bullshits about another so-called 'headmaid' hiring her, acting all confused. The woman is also confused (fair) and goes to head downstairs, so Helwyn clocks her, dragging her unconscious body back to the party (that's sort of a success?)
- Back to Izek's room! Helwyn picks the lock on the door (Martyn makes this look way too easy!) and peaks inside. Izek and Ismark are curled up in bed, although it's probably not consentual what with Ismark being gagged and hog-tied. The group discuss how exactly they're going to handle this: Dirge will long-cast Silence around the two to keep the noise down; Mother will go in and get Ismark out of Izek's embrace; Arran will ready Phantasmal Killer to fuck with Izek in case he wakes up and Helwyn will watch for other staff down the hallway. A perfect plan, right?
## We Fuck This Up
- Oh right, it's us! As Mother tries to quietly cut Ismark free of his binds, the sleeping giant places his hand onto her arm. She tries to move it away without him noticing, but he wakes up and is NOT HAPPY. Arran fires off Phantasmal Killer which succeeds (YAY), but turns out Izek gets advantage on attacks when afraid (SHIT). Izek creates a block of fire in the hallway where Arran and Dirge are standing, which might alert the rest of the house that something's up?
- Arran gets inside the room and bravely takes on Izek, but Izek grabs him by the head and tosses him through two walls and outside the house. Arran manages to Misty Step back into the house before he hits the ground, like the wizard version of a ninja (winja?)
- Helwyn's alerted to the plan going sideways thanks to Arran making his own doors and windows. She dives into the room and somehow manages to drag Ismark out via the hole Arran made (I like to think the maid disguise is still in full effect). Mother gets slammed through the floor and ceiling before being thrown into a corner of the room (Okay, the Baron's people DEFINITELY know something's up now).
- Izek moves up to the gang in the hallway. Dirge spots the weak spot at the shoulder where his arm joins the rest of him. Helwyn attacks that spot, damaging the arm and infuriating Izek, who calmly responds by throwing Helwyn back through to the kid's room via *his* room (calling it now, this building is no longer structurally sound).
- Baron and Vasili come upstairs, with Baron confused and screaming for Izek to kill these intruders. Vasili checks with us if complications should be dealt with. Dirge says yep, so Vasili stabs the Baron straight through the chest and leaves. I guess that helps in a way? Arran cuts Ismark's arms free and he ungags himself. He says that the Sun Sword was handed off to someone on the way to the Baron's house (SHIT AGAIN).
## Un-Irish Goodbye
- Arran and Ismark jump out of the hole down to the ground. Mother disengages and gets out of there too, with Dirge following behind. Helwyn jumps out of the kid's window, but Izek throws his axe into her shoulder as she does (ow). She floats down to the ground on what will shortly be known as the non-dangerous side of the house.
- Izek follows the rest of the party out of the hole and attacks them. Doru turns up as he'd seen the pillar of smoke from outside the town (makes sense). We say he can eat Izek, so a delighted Doru bites the fella and they teleport 60 feet straight up into the air to drop him (a baller move for sure). Izek grabs Doru and sends him slamming through the house to the other side (not so baller).
- Helwyn heads around the house and flies up to Izek as he falls, getting another attack in on the arm as Izek comes down and hits the ground. He takes it like a champ, calmly exploding outwards with a fire burst that hits pretty much everyone. Helwyn almost dies and falls out of the air, but she's caught by Mother at the last second and is still up somehow. What a lucky escape!
## Paying the Price
- Izek temper-tantrums more further fire explosions, taking down Dirge, Mother and Helwyn. Arran's the only one still standing, and he manages to fire Magic Missiles point-blank into Izek's arm join, finally taking the big bastard down (BADASS).
- Arran manages to stabilise Mother, but finds that Helwyn and Dirge have died from their battle wounds. Doru returns and Arran asks him to try and bring back Dirge, but the only way he can do that is by making Dirge undead. Arran makes the call for him to do it (Dirge is going to be in a interesting space after this, that's for sure).
- Arran goes to Helwyn's body and grabs the key that Glasya had given her as part of the contract. He invokes Glasya's name, telling her that if she doesn't want it going to Strahd, she better help bring Helwyn come back right now (never mind, THAT is badass). Let's see if a certain archdevil pays Arran a visit...
# TL;DR
- Mother receives a letter inviting the party to dinner with a Lady Fiona Wachter. She also bumps into Izek and (not realising who he is) leads him to Ismark at the church, leaving the two of them to come find the rest of the party. When they realise what's happened, the party heads to the church and finds the aftermath of a fight with no sign of Izek or Ismark.
- They follow the smell of blood via Dirge's trusty mount Rosemary to the Baron's house. As they debate how to handle getting Ismark back, they are interrupted by Vasili Von Holtz, a fellow hater of Strahd and vampire-killer who says that he can help us get Ismark back and gives us some useful intel.
- We break into the second floor of the Baron's house while Visili creates a distraction at the front door. We find Ismark bound by a (now sleeping) Izek and attempt to get him out quietly, but everything goes sideways. A massive fight breaks out between the party and Izek with multiple people thrown through walls and fiery attacks abound.
- Eventually, Arran manages to land the killing blow on Izek, but Helwyn and Dirge have lost their lives in the process. Arran takes Glasya's key and invokes her name, demanding her help to bring Helwyn back or he'll pass it along to Strahd.
