# What Actually Happened?
## Changing Gears
- While Arran, Helwyn, Dirge and Martyn are off investigating for Father Lucien, Mother has been working in the Blue Water Inn's kitchen for... some reason. Urwin mentions to her that a scruffy-looking man in a brown cloak had brought a letter addressed to her. Inside the letter is a dinner invitation to the party from one Lady Fiona Wachter of Wachterhaus. More out-of-the-blue dinners with people we don't know - gotta go better than last time, right?
- An exhausted and fairly beaten-up Martyn comes storming into the inn with some surprisingly good-quality wood planks and makes his way to the suite, clearly in a bad mood. Mother salutes him as he passes (wise choice, probably best not getting caught in THAT storm right now).
- Mother heads out into the town to try and locate the rest of the party. She bumps into Izek, who says he's been looking for the "blonde twink boy" AKA Ismark. When she asks why, Izek says that he's really into Ismark and would like to see him. Mother thinks nothing of why a giant man with a monstrous arm might be looking for an enemy of Strahd, so of course she offers to take him to the church to find Ismark.
- Once they get to the church, Ismark is visibly surprised that Mother has brought Izek to find him, slowly reaching for his sword. She reads the tension as a relationship blossoming rather than a shitshow about to kick off, so she heads out to give the two privacy and find the rest of the party.
## Beat 2
- Speaking of shitshows: After the aftermath of the coffin shop, Father Lucien has offered to provide us a blessing from the church for the work we did in helping him, but he has to perform the rites while consecrating the bones of St. Andral. Dirge thinks that Father Lucien has no idea if this'll work, and he's right: it's the first time Lucien has done it, so we're guinea pigs for his little holy experiment. Fun! 
- When we get 15 minutes away from the church, the party (minus Martyn who's sleeping off the fight and Jaswyn who is... somewhere?) finally come back together. Mother tells us about how she found a man who wanted to find Ismark (uh oh.) He was also taller than her and had a giant arm (UH OH).
- Quickly rushing back to the church, we find that everything is smashed to pieces. Clearly a fight happened here, but there's no bodies. We do however find some blood that Mother analyses by tasting it (ew) and determines that it's not Ismark's blood. Ismark must have tried to slice up Izek, but Izek probably never needed or chose to draw a weapon. Then again, that battering ram he calls an arm is pretty much all the weapon he probably needs.
## Beat 3
- Dirge gets his hound/mount Rosemary to sniff the blood and see if she can find out where the owner's gone. She picks up the scent and we follow it, eventually turning up outside the Baron's house. On the way, Mother mentions about the invitation that we received. Some of us are sceptical about being invited to another dinner hosted by an unknown person, but Arran *really* wants a fancy dinner. He asks Helwyn not to ruin this one (Hey!) She doesn't know how fancy dinners are meant to work, but she knows an asshole lord when she sees one. Arran tells her to play the game more, but she doesn't make any promises.
- While we're talking, a man comments on how interesting our party is. He's been standing there for 20 minutes listening to us. He asks if we hate Strahd, to which we reply "Who's asking?" He introduces himself as Vasili Von Holtz, a nobleman from up north, and says that he hates vampires and has killed three vampire nobles so far - impressive!
- He asks if we know who killed Volenta. Obviously not us haha! We talk around it a little and after some beating around the bush, Dirge reckoning he's trustworthy, we take the conversation into a secluded alleyway and he opens the bag to reveal three severed heads (disgusting). Dirge offers to prestidigitate them to get rid of the smell, but Vasili says he likes it (more disgusting).
- He tells that he saw us kill Volenta, but wanted to make sure that we didn't just spill the beans to just anybody, as we wouldn't be worth working with against Strahd. He says that he can help with getting Ismark back, as he's been watching the Baron's house for about a week now. He knows how to get Izek's room on the top floor via the Baron's kid's room. Vasili's been watching him, but not in a creepy way: The kid has arcane powers and is trying to become a lich.
