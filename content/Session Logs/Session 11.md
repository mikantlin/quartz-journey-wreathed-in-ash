# What Actually Happened?
## A Grave Danger
- We get Ismark's deceased loved ones to the Church of [[Ezra|the Mists]], where prayer can be heard within. We knock but no-one responds. [[Parriwimble]] turns up and says that [[Father Donavich]] is scared and refusing to answer. Dirge does his undead ping and picks up something in the church basement. [[Ismark]] asks Parriwimble to knock, which he does NOT fuck around on, ringing the church's bells and causing Father Donavich to call for us to come in
- When we get in, Dirge asks him about the undead in the basement. He tells us that his son [[Doru]] is locked in there, having come back to the town as a vampire spawn, but that he cannot bring himself to kill Doru. He asks to do it, and we agree to it. Ismark offers to wait with Donavich, and [[Usi-Ziyad|Usi]] offers to wait with [[Ravaria]], which is... nice, I guess?
- We go down into the basement and find Doru unlike the feral vampire spawn that we were expecting. He wants to help us with taking down [[Strahd]], and so Dirge offers his blood to see if Doru can control his abilities. A couple of the party are sceptical, but Dirge says that "we don't kill victims" (badass). Doru drinks from the cleric and regains his strength, using his faith to hold onto his willpower and resist his curse.
- He tells us that 16 weeks ago, he encountered a man called Alanic Ray protecting the town from a werewolf(?) attack. During the attack, his disguise hat is knocked off and reveals him as [[Rudolph Van Richten|Van Richten]], who Doru nerds out over having read all of his book
- Doru, Van Richten and [[Escher]] lead a rebellion against [[Strahd]]. They open the Moonbearer's coffin to find it empty, and Doru realises that Escher has vanished. Van Richten escaped, but Doru was captured and tortured by [[Rahadin]] for Van R's whereabouts over the next three months. Strahd eventually released Doru and commanded him to kill Father Donavich 
- He tells us that [[Madame Eva]] and her witches should be able to help him cut the ties to his master using their river of blood (yuck). He also lets us know about VR's weapons cache that could help us fight vampires, hidden under a great oak along the river (the normal one, not the blood one). After some conversation, we finally agree to take him with us on the agreement that we lop his head off if he gets bitey with us
## Funeral for a Friend('s Uncle and Sister)
# TL;DR
- We confront Doru, the leader of the rebellion against Strahd, now his vampire spawn. With some control over his abilities, we decide to bring Doru with us to find a freedom from his Strahd connection
- After some arguing, we decide to go along with the plan to call Rahadin to the town center and ambush him there with Usi's assistance
- On enacting the plan, things go sideways as Ludmilla (who is actually Usi's ex-wife) arrives as well. Usi teleports her and his daughter(!) Ravaria out of the action as Ismark is stabbed by Rahadin
- We fight like crazy to bring down Rahadin as Usi's Order gets the townsfolk out. Usi sets the town on fire on returning as Ismark almost kills Ludmilla and hurts Rahadin badly. Ludmilla gets away as Rahadin releases his 1000 tormentors on us
