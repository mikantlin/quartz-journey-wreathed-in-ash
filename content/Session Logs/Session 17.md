# What Actually Happened?
## Damage Control
- [[Glasya]] offers a deal to [[Martyn]]: his soul for taking his destructive power away, but Martyn turns it down. So she lets Martyn explode again.
- As the blast reaches us, time slows as it destroys [[Arran]]'s arm (and possibly kills [[Jaswyn]] too). The blast suddenly freezes into ice. A figure with a snake walks past nonchalantly. They introduce themselves as [[Loftur]] and explain that they're a traveller through this realm who's fascinated in our story and doesn't want it to end.
- Loftur reduces the Martyn blast back into Martyn and shrinks Glasya to a tiny size, making her (mostly) harmless. He also stops Arran from exploding with elemental energy, instead reducing him down to a pearl-like object of energy. [[Dirge]] puts him in one of his belt jars for now, and Loftur... vanishes? I don't know, [[Helwyn]]'s zoning out.
- The party talked to Arran about what it’s like to be ANOTHER bomb, although this time elemental. Helwyn snaps back to reality after about 20 minutes, and making the most of the opportunity, we head to the Hag's Arms.
## Knock, Knock
- As we reach the steps up to the place, we recall being given advice to introduce ourselves before entering a hag's home. We don't really know where the boundary is though, so to play it safe Helwyn calls out from the bottom of the steps that she's here to ask for her soul back. That should work, right?
- As we head up the steps, hundreds of giant insects crawl out and watch us ascend. Creepy... We reach the Hag's Arms and enter. We call out for the hag and wouldn't you know it, [[Baba Yaga]]  comes straight to us!
- She asks us to follow her to her basement. Helwyn senses that her soul is contained within the hag, so she goes along. All the while, time's a-ticking...
## Hag(gling)
- Baba Yaga will only give Helwyn's soul back in exchange for other parts of bodies. People start negotiating body parts, with eyes and hands being offered up. Helwyn HATES it.
- As the seconds are almost up, Helwyn impulsively offers up the party dealing with Glasya. She's been a thorn in Baba Yaga's side, so she agrees to let us out of Gehenna if we bring her Glasya's heart. Easy peasy! Baba Yaga says that we will also need a weapon designed specifically for dealing with Glasya, and gives [[M0-TH3R|Mother]] the [[Scourge of Shadows]].
- She lets us long rest in her lair, so we don't suffer from Gehenna's influence over us. When we wake up, we surprisingly feel refreshed. Baba Yaga tells us to head towards Glasya's fortress while keeping the desire of fighting Glasya in her mind, and that the weapon will bring her to us.
- Arran negotiates for his curse to be dealt with, but Baba Yaga can't remove it, only suppress it. Baba Yaga gives him some tea, keeping his curse at bay for 8 hours (better than nothing I guess). She also lets him know that it is Baba Lasagna (something like that anyway) who was the one to curse him. Off we go!
## Eyes on the Prize
- We head out and bump into Loftur, who offers to escort us back to the Material Plane. When we say we can't, he calls us all idiots for making a deal with the hag
- He also negotiates with Martyn around a new eye in exchange for Martyn's bomb powers. They haggle and eventually agree to loan to each other for seven months, so Martyn's not blowing up any time soon (by himself anyway)
- We head towards Glasya's fortress and think about fighting her. She appears (no longer a tiny mouse devil, dammit) and pretty much one-shots Helwyn down into the ground. We manage to hold our own surprisingly well for a fight that's hilariously out of our league, but it's only a matter of time before resources run dry and Glasya gets the upper hand...
# TL;DR
- A mysterious entity called Loftur traps Martyn's explosion in ice and controls Arran's elemental outburst too, saving our lives. He asks for nothing in return and offers to get us out of Gehenna. As our deadline grows closer, we make our way to the Hag's Arms and find Baba Yaga.
- Negotiations are desperately made with Baba Yaga, with Helwyn offering to kill Glasya for Baba Yaga. She agrees to helping the party leave if we bring her Glasya's heart and gives us a powerful artefact to fight her with. She also takes some eyes in exchange for the deal, but returns Helwyn's soul to her.
- After a long rest, we bump into Loftur outside, who makes a deal with Martyn to loan a new eye in exchange for borrowing his arcane power. We make our way towards Glasya but get teleported to her fortress as per Yaga's instructions. We immediately go into the fight of our lives against a powerful archdevil, with no apparent light at the end of the tunnel.
