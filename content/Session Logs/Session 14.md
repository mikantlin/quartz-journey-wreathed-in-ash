# What Actually Happened?
## Rule of Thumb
- The party's talking to Rictavio (AKA [[Doctor Arthur Sedwick|Dr Arthur Sedwick]]) in the stables, encouraging him to tell stories of rebellion to the masses outside the gates (no particular reason, don't read into it). He workshops some ideas, like maybe an evil Baron Thumb oppressing the city he rules? [[Ismark the Lesser|Ismark]] interrupts our creative writing brainstorming session to ask us to speak to [[Father Lucien]] about an incident he needs help dealing with.
- As we come out, we see a procession coming down the street. At the head of it is [[Baron Vargas Vallakovich]], flanked on either side by two dogs. Two *big* dogs. I'm talking *round*. Anyway, an old woman gets pushed out from an alleyway in front of the parade, the horses halting just feet away. The Baron's henchman/lackey/advisor(?) [[Izek]] follows her out and things get tense. This calls for heroics!
- We rush to the woman before she can be harmed further, who is being detained by guards and is screaming for her son [[Udo]] to be released from the boot camp. [[Dirge]] asks the Baron just what the hell's going on. The Baron explains that her son has been suffering from "malicious unhappiness" and she seems to have caught it too (can't imagine why in a town this fun!) He tasks the guards to shove her outside. Dirge rolls for the shit-for-brains equivalent of Eyes of the Grave and gets a ping off the Baron. We argue with him some more, and he eventually settles for kindly putting her in the stocks for four days instead.
- The baron heads off and Izek says that if we want to speak to the Baron, we need to go through him (and there's a lot of him to go through.) Dirge doesn't take his shit, and things get heated between them before Father Lucien intervenes, asking for peace. Izek pats him on the head and moves off. Yeah, you better run!
- We discuss/debate the town politics with Lucien (way too openly in [[Helwyn]]'s opinion), before he asks us to help with some damage to the church. The town's coffin maker, [[Henrik]], is already working on it, but Father Lucien clearly recognises our group's interior decorating skills. At the very least it's a more private space, so the gang head over there.
## CSI: Knafgata
- As we get to the church doors, Henrik is heading back to his shop in the Stockyards. He drops his stuff all over the place - apparently he can't find his eyeglasses (which feels like not a great time to be doing woodworking, but what do I know?)
- We see a young priest, [[Milivoj]], fixing the window with boy, [[Yeska]], sweeping up the remains. Milivoj gets *real* defensive of us coming in (suspicious, if you ask me). The gang ask him a few questions before he heads off. According to Lucien, Milivoj is six months new to the [[Knafgata]] priesthood. He spent some time over the border, but Lucien says he's legit: I mean, he's got robes, knows the holy words and everything! Milivoj lives in the north-west part of town. Helwyn's pretty sure this guy was involved in [[Yrsa]]'s murder, so she can't wait to get to ~~torture and murder him~~ ask some follow-up questions!
- Father Lucien gets real with us about what actually happened: the night before, someone broke into the crypt and stole the [[Bones of St. Andral]], a holy relic that protects the church from fiends. He refreshes the bones with divine power every year, at the beginning of the feast of St. Andral (makes sense). The next feast is in four days, so without the bones the church is a prime target for, say, vampiric shenanigans.
- Down into the crypt we go! Lucien has the main key, and he also hid a spare on a necklace he gave to Yeska, just in case. Inside, [[Arran Tirran|Arran]] and Helwyn find no evidence of foul play. Meanwhile, [[Martyn]] and Dirge, thinking they're better at investigating than us, find clumps of earth in the shape of bootprints with white flower petals, specifically ramsen flowers that grow in the church's graveyard. Also, strands of dark-black hair. Oh, and rust-stained dirt. A scrap of grey wool, too. And the wooden ceiling has exploded outwards, as if something has burst out from the crypt, but on closer inspection it's been tampered with to *look* like that (Alright fine, Cap and Cleric *are* better at this).
- Lucien mentions that he had told Yeska about the bones' existence to calm his fears of being in danger, so we ask to talk to the kid. His parents died 3 months ago, so Lucien advises some tact might be in order (that's Arran and Helwyn out). Arran uses Detect Thoughts to be able to read Yeska's surface-level thoughts though. We debate about the ethics of using it on a kid. Helwyn takes the strong stance of not giving a shit either way. Martyn's thought that Dirge was a child this entire time (I have no idea exactly how this came up, but it's very important information!)
- Dirge asks Yeska about the crypt, which he doesn't even know exists - turns out he thought it was the Father's office. He wasn't at the church the previous day as he was sick (he gets a fever about once a month, because of the graveyard air). He often helps with the church stuff like digging graves, etc. with Milivoj, who's has been telling Yeska about [[Strahd]] coming to attack the church. Stories that Milivoj finds funny (classic dick teenager behaviour). No more leads here, so guessing we need to have a word with Priest Boy!
## Kids These Days
- Lucien leads us in the direction of Milivoj's home. There's dirt on the porch, and Martyn tastes it using his expert mud knowledge: definitely the same dirt as the stuff in the crypt. Dirge and Helwyn deciding to head around the back in case he runs and so that Helwyn doesn't spook him. Martyn and Arran are met by [[Jazinka]], a glassy-eyed 70 year old with 3 toddlers and 2 teenagers inside the house, 7 kids overall! (Apparently she got her eggs frozen one town over. Milivoj took them. Don't ask.)
- Milivoj comes out the back with 3 toddlers attached to his limbs. Helwyn and Dirge stop him and ask him some questions (Helwyn's a bit asshole-ish about it). He tries to claim he saw a cloaked figure take them, but almost immediately reveals that he stole the bones for Henrik, figuring that no-one would really miss some dead guy anyway and that the magic stuff is just made-up crap. Henrik had asked for the relics as St. Andral was (apparently) his ancestor and his family had been searching for them for centuries. Milivoj had told scary stories to Yeska so that Father Lucien would tell him about the bones' protection and confirm they were in the church (sorta clever?) Milivoj reckoned he could frame Izek for the crime (less clever, that guy does NOT look like he would take being framed well.)
- Helwyn asks him about his work prior to the priesthood. Milivoj has done any kind of job to get money for his family, including mercenary work out East. He ran with a group for a few months, but didn't like the actions they took, so he moved on. Damn... Helwyn is conflicted about her quest, but getting the bones back seem to be more important right now, so they head off for the coffin shop.
- We make our way to the Arasek Stockyard. On the way, we pass a cart with a freshly-painted sign saying "Rictavio's Carnival of Wonders" painted on the side. Hmmm, mental note for later.
- At the shop, we see Henrik through a kitchen window and knock on the door. Henrik jumps and drops a knife into his foot. Sigh. Martyn picks the lock and we go in, Helwyn healing Henrick's foot even though he doesn't deserve. We ask about the bones and Henrik tells us that a woman in a yellow dress with a skull mask demanded that he get them for, and she didn't ask in a nice way. Well shit, that sounds like [[Volenta]] alright.
- Henrick tells us the bones are upstairs and that Volenta's on her way to collect them (FUCK), so Dirge books it upstairs. He barely manages to avoid poison darts rigged at the top of the stairs, but stumbles into a tripwire attached to a creepy doll. Helwyn follows after him and the commotion he's making, noticing the sound of something large skittering on the ceiling of the dark room (nopenopenopenope...)
## Hammer Time
- Dirge turns to see Volenta right behind Helwyn, upside-down on the ceiling. Helwyn takes a wild swing, barely avoiding a hammer blow to the back of the head. 3 vampire spawn appear and the fight is on. At first, none of our attacks on Volenta are landing, her teleporting out of the way each time, until Martyn spots her necklace glowing on each attack. We focus our efforts there, Helwyn smashing the necklace off Volenta and getting fucking bit for her efforts.
- Volenta moves fast, not even really using her hammer, but the cleric boys' Spirit Guardians wipe out the vampire spawn. Dirge breaks the creepy doll so that no more appear. Martyn jumps to where the bones are hidden and in true chaos style, attempts to Thunderwave the floor to force the bones out that way. Arran jumps in and attempts to stab the floor as well, completely missing it despite it being, you know, a floor.
- Martyn decides to go for desperate tactics and grabs the bones, diving out of the window and into the alleyway below. He eats dirt (not for the first time this session), falling unconscious. Volenta, being on the ropes, drops a vine bomb to ensnare us, followed by a firebomb to flambé us, before jumping out the window and bringing her hammer down on the back of Martyn's head. His eye pops out and Volenta tears it from his head (welcome to the club, buddy!)
- Volenta books it over the rooftops. Arran and Dirge Misty Step in pursuit, while Helwyn can't get out of these DAMN FUCKING VINES. Arran hits Volenta with a couple of magic missile attacks, bringing her down as Dirge steps up. Volenta swears that Strahd will make us pay before Dirge finishes her off for good with a Sacred Flame to the face. Clerics, man.
- Helwyn finally gets free and sees Martyn down in the alleyway bleeding to death. She jumps down and provides some healing, bringing him back but breaking the news that he's part of the One-Eye Gang now. Angrily, he heads into Henrik's shop and grabs the fanciest wooden planks he can find. However you need to process, my guy! So that's one of Strahd's wives brought to near-death and one that's, like, *dead* dead. The Moonbearer is REALLY not going to be happy about this...

# TL;DR
- The gang gets their first introduction to the Baron of Knafgata and his lackey Izek, when an old woman is almost crushed, then exiled, for being unhappy. Father Lucien intervenes, taking us to the church and asking us to investigate a robbery of a powerful relic: The bones of St. Andral.
- We met Yeska, a young orphan who helps at the church, and Milivoj, a fairly recent addition to the Knafgata priesthood.  We check out the crime scene and speak to Yeska, ultimately learning that he didn't have any reason to steal the relic or even know the crypt that housed it existed.
- We went to speak with Milivoj's house. Catching him outside with three of his seven siblings, we ask him about the theft and he quickly admits to it, saying that Henrik the coffin maker paid him for the bones as they were a family heirloom. He also shared that he had done mercenary work in the East, leading Helwyn to believe that he was in the mercenary gang who killed Yrsa.
- Heading to the coffin maker's shop, we confront Henrik. He admits that a woman matching Volenta's description demanded he get the bones and that she was on her way. With Dirge and Helwyn heading upstairs to find them, they're ambushed by Volenta. After a tense fight between us, her and three vampire spawn, she almost gets away before Dirge gets in the killing blow.
