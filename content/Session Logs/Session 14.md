# What Actually Happened?
- We talk to Rictavio in the stables, encourage him to tell stories of rebellion
- Ismark comes in and asks us to speak to Father Lucien about an incident he'd like us to deal with, and that Ismark's off to handle something else
- As we come out, we see a parade down the street. Baron with two massively fat dogs
- An old woman is pushed out from an alleyway in front of the parade, halting feet away. Izek follows out from the alleyway
- We rush to the woman and ask the Baron what's going on. He says that she is clearly suffering from malicious unhappiness and needs to be put outside. We argue with him, and he settles for putting her in the stocks for four days instead
- The woman screams for the baron to release her son Udo from the boot camp
- The baron heads off and Izek says that if we want to speak to the Baron, we need to go through him. Things between him and Dirge get heated
- Father Lucien intervenes, asking for peace. Izek pats him on the head and moves off. We discuss some things with Lucien (way too openly). He asks us to help with a falling tree branch that smashed a window in the church, so we head there to talk more privately
- As we get to the temple doors, the coffinmaker Henrik Vandervot is heading out. He is heading back to his shop in the Stockyards. He drops his stuff, apparently he can't find his eyeglasses and is struggling with it. Helwyn helps him with his things
- As we head in, we see a young priest, Milavoj, fixing the window with boy, Yeska, sweeping up the remains. Milavoj is six months new here, and very defensive of us coming in. We ask him a couple of things and he leaves. Helwyn is pretty sure this guy was involved in Yrsa's murder. Father Lucien says that he lives in the north-west part of town
- Father Lucien shares what has actually happened: the night before, someone broke into the crypt and stole the bones of St. Andril, a holy relic that protects the temple from fiends with a hallowed spell
- Father Lucien refreshes the bones with divine power every year, at the beginning of the feast of St. Andril. The next feast is in four days, so without the bones the temple is in jeopardy
- He mentions that he had told Yeska about the bones' existence to calm his fears of being in danger
- He leads us down into the crypt. He has a key, and he also hid one on a necklace he gave to Yeska, just in case. Inside the crypt, Arran and Helwyn find fuck all, while Martyn and the priest find clumps of earth in the shape of bootprints with white flower petals, specifically ramsen flowers that grow in the church's graveyard. We also find strands of dark-black hair, rust-stained dirt and a scrap of grey wool. The wooden ceiling has exploded outwards, as if something has burst out from the crypt.
- We ask to talk to Yeska. Father Lucien warns us that his parents died 3 months ago, so some tact might be in order. Arran uses Detect Thoughts to be able to read Yeska's surface-level thoughts (also, Martyn has thought Dirge was a child this entire time!)
- We ask Yeska about the crypt, which he doesn't know about. He wasn't at the temple the previous day as he was sick (he gets a fever about once a month, he thinks because of the graveyard air). He often helps with the church stuff like digging graves, etc. with Milavoj. Milavoj has been telling Yeska about Strahd coming to attack the church, stories that Milavoj finds funny.
- We ask Lucien about Milavoj. Apparently, he joined the church 6 months' ago. He'd served as a priest of Astra (new god) in Nionaem, knew the rites, and has priest's robes, so that was good enough for Lucien
- Lucian leads us in the direction of Milavoj's home. There's dirt on the porch, and Martyn tastes it using his expert mud knowledge: definitely the same dirt as the stuff in the crypt. Dirge and Helwyn deciding to head around the back in case he runs and so that Helwyn doesn't spook him. Martyn and Arran are met by Jazinka, a glassy-eyed 70 year old with 3 toddlers and 2 teenagers inside the house. 7(!) kids overall, apparently she got her eggs frozen in whatever way that works in this world
- Milavoj comes out the back with 3 toddlers attached to his limbs. Helwyn and Dirge stop him and ask him some questions (Helwyn's a bit heavy-handed/assholish about it). He tries to claim he saw a cloaked figure take them, but eventually just reveals that he stole the bones for Henrik, figuring that no-one would really miss them. Henrik had asked for them as he claimed that St Andril was his ancestor and his family had been searching for them for centuries. Milavoj had told scary stories to Yeska so that Father Lucian would tell him about the bones' protection and confirm they were in the church. Milavoj reckoned he could frame Izek for the crime
- Helwyn asks him about his work prior to the priesthood. Milavoj has done any kind of job to get money for his family, including mercenary work out East. He ran with a group for a few months, but didn't like the actions they took, so he left. Helwyn is conflicted about her quest, but getting the bones back seem to be more important right now
- We head to the Arasek Stockyard where Henrik's coffin shop is. On the way, we pass a cart with a freshly-painted sign saying "Rictavio's Carnival of Wonders" painted on the side. Keep a note of that
- When we get to the shop, we see Henrik through a kitchen window and knock on the door. Henrik jumps and drops a knife into his foot. Sigh. Martyn picks the lock and we go in, Helwyn healing Henrick's foot. We ask about the bones and Henrik tells us that a woman in a yellow dress with a skull mask demanded that he get them for, and she didn't ask in a nice way. Sounds like Volenta alright
- Henrick tells us the bones are upstairs and that Volenta's on her way to collect them, so Dirge books it upstairs. He manages to just avoid poison darts, but stumbles into a tripwire attached to a creepy doll. Helwyn follows the commotion and hears skittering, with Dirge turning to see Volenta behind Helwyn, upside down on the ceiling. Helwyn takes a wild swing, then barely avoids a hammer blow to the head. Looks like we're fighting!
- 3 vampire spawn appear and the fight is on. At first, none of our attacks on Volenta are landing, her teleporting out of the way each time, until Martyn spots her necklace glowing on each attack. We focus our efforts there, Helwyn smashing the necklace off Volenta and getting bit in return
- Volenta moves fast, not even really using her hammer, but the cleric boys' Spirit Guardians wipe out the vampire spawn. Dirge breaks the creepy doll so that no more appear. Martyn jumps to where the bones are hidden and in true chaos style, attempts to Thunderwave the floor to force the bones out that way. Arran jumps in and attempts to stab the floor as well, completely missing it despite, you know, it being a floor
- Martyn decides to go for desperate tactics and grabs the bones, diving out of the window and into the alleyway below. He eats dirt (not for the first time this session), falling unconscious. Volenta, being on the ropes, drops a vine bomb to ensnare us, followed by a firebomb to flambé us, before jumping out the window and bringing her hammer down on the back of Martyn's head. His eye pops out and Volenta tears it from his head (welcome to the club Martyn!)
- Volenta books it over the rooftops. Arran and Dirge misty step and persue, while Helwyn can't get out of these damn fucking vines. Arran hits Volenta with a couple of magic missile attacks, bringing her down as Dirge steps up. Volenta swears that Strahd will make us pay before Dirge finishes her off for good. Clerics, man
- Helwyn finally gets free and sees Martyn down outside the window. She jumps down and provides some healing, breaking the news that he's part of the One-Eye Gang now. Angrily, he heads into Henrik's shop and grabs the fanciest wooden planks he can find. However you need to process, my guy
