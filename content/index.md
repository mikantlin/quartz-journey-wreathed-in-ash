_First came the fires. Then came the rot. Now, something deeper stirs, beneath the soil, beneath the skin, beneath the world. Villages vanish in smoke. Forests twist into mockeries of themselves. Water turns foul and those who drink it suffer fates worse than death. In the shadows of these disasters, a symbol is always found, carved into stone, burned into walls, scratched into flesh. A single, searing rune._

_Infected settlements suffer hallucinations, hysteria, and a strange compulsion to wander. Forests weep blood. Children are born without eyes, yet speak languages no one remembers. A secretive cult fans the flames of this rot, marking locations for ritual corruption, keeping a cadre of demons in place long enough to warp reality itself._

_If they succeed, the land will never recover. It will become a permanent wound. An open gate to the Abyss. You are not heroes. You are the last light in a world that doesn’t realize it’s already smoldering. Track their symbols. Uncover their rites. Burn their temples before the land itself begins to breathe. But beware. Every step deeper into this nightmare marks you._

_The fire watches. The Rune remembers. The Abyss isn’t coming._

_It’s already here._
