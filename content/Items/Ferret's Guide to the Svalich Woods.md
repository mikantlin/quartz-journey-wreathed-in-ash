# Welcome!
With all the ceremony I want to dedicate this book to [[Lady Arisa von Fellengard]] and to all, all, all my friends.

My name is [[Ferret]] and I am the author of this book! It was a present for Lady Arisa von Fellengard during our wonderful vacation in [[Krezk]], so if you accidently happened to find it, please try and give it back!

But if she or any of my other friends lend it to you, then **<u>a big, big welcome!</u>**
Now I will introduce myself properly: I'm 10 years old, the [[Seeker]]'s daughter and the priestess of the [[Mother Night]]!

All my life I have lived in the [[Svalich Woods]] and have seen many, many interesting things there, which I wrote down for my friends and anyone else who might find the stories amusing or helpful.

(~~this book is a bit like the one by Van Richten, but I think mine is still better~~)
I hope the book will make you happy and cheer you up!

With love and all sorts of nice things

\- Ferret.
# Catch Me
It can be very, very lonely and boring in the woods. During the day, here's no one to talk to or play with, and in the evening you have to go hide in a burrow. But I know a few tricks to avoid being all alone!

For example, if you start running fast in a thicket, after a while a curious creature will start chasing you.

They look just like normal people, only with strange arms and legs they use to run really fast. And their eyes are so huge to better watch you and your movements.

First, only one appears, but the longer you run, the more of them there are!

The first time we met, they really scared me. I thought they were trying to chase me the same way wolves chase rabbits. But just when I got too tired to run any more, I noticed a burrow and hid inside.

After catching my breath, I peeked out of my hiding and saw that nobody was running after me anymore. That's when I understood, they were just playing!

Afterwards, I started running in the thickets on purpose, because running with friends is so much more fun than running alone!
# Talkative Flowers
I think the world is full of beautiful things, but sometimes it's hard to invent new stuff and you imitate what you like.

I think this is why these flowers look like human heads, since humans are very beautiful!

The patterns on the pistils look like human eyes and the petals look like a mouth (through which you can see the seeds).

The flowers are very polite and happy to help. If you talk to them, they will answer in your head, first quietly, then louder.

Mostly they ask to eat a seed and sometimes ask to do other things, but Mommy said if someone insists on you doing something, then you should not do it.

That's why you should talk to them only a little, because after a few minutes your head starts to hurt and you feel sleepy.
# The Moaning Tree
That's what I call a small tree that I found deep in the woods when I was walking around with ravens to find myself something to eat. If you bury a seed or some food under that tree, it will instantly grow whatever you buried on its branches.

Of course, I was very happy about this discovery, because it's not that easy to find a lot of food! Although sometimes the tree grows things a bit wrong, and after eating a couple of buns from it, my hands began to shake and something red came out of my eyes. The ravens were very angry with me and I had to sleep for many many days before getting better.

After that I went to the tree only once, and there was a woman. Instead of buns and vegetables, she was burying bones. Her hands were visibly shaking and her face was hidden under a hood, so I think she wasn't doing it for the first time.

After a while the tree grew something horrible and crying. I didn't like it at all and I think, Mommy didn't like it either. The woman fell dead on the spot, but the moaning and screaming from the tree wouldn't stop. I decided to run away and leave it be, so I can only guess what happened next.
# The Sitting One
The Sitting One looks like an ancient grandma, but the lower half of her body grew deep into the trunk of the tree on which she sits. She is almost entirely covered with moss and branches, but her face remains clear, even if her eyes look muddy.

I think she can't move, but also she doesn't want to.

For example, her arms freely lie on her lap and even though they are not covered by roots, the Sitting One does not move them: From her trunk giant roots reach into the far distance. I don't think they do anything special.

Once I saw how hunters came across the Sitting One. One of them took out his weapon, but then suddenly got scared and started talking about a lot of things in his life. The other hunter got angry because of that, but before he could attack his friend, he unwillingly also started talking about himself. In the end they started fighting each other, until one of them tried to attack the Sitting One. Immediately after that he started twitching scarily and fell dead. The other one ended the same way.

Once I went up to her to offer food and accidentally told her a lot of things which I never wanted to tell anyone. I cried because of that, but then she pet my head, comforting me

Mommy said that the Sitting One just likes honest people, but it's better not to come near her, since not only she can hear what you say in the forest.

Still sometimes I visit her so that she doesn't feel lonely.
# The Sad Ones
Sometimes things just don't go well in the forest, so you just die there.

But some dislike it so much that their spirit stays there.

Because they upset me a lot, they never linger near my burrow for long, but I still meet the Sad Ones on my forest adventures!

They don't do anything at all besides crying over the place where they have died. I tried comforting them before, but it seemed like they didn't even see me.

But one time, the Sad One seemingly confused me for someone and started reaching out to me. Suddenly, one of Mommy's ravens appeared and flew towards her. Sadly, it died at that exact moment, while the Sad One vanished.

After burying the raven I decided not to approach the Sad Ones again.

Light is something they don't like at all, so they only live in the dark parts of the forest.
# The Friendly One
I'm not really sure if they're a man or a woman, but whoever they may be, they know one of the most important rules of friendship! Which is, that you have to be generous and make great presents.

They have neither eyes nor a mouth, nor a nose, nor even clothing!

They look more like a human-like shape, but they're not bothered by it at all. And also, they have very thick skin, so even when someone tries to stab or hit them, they don't care!

They don't pay attention to me or to other animals and Mommy says it's because they don't realise I'm human.

But they look for other humans and try to become friends with them. Sometimes they give someone a jolly smile and that person walks away very happy, but sometimes things don't work out so well and they give away one eye or nose too many.

But when you're friends, you have to make gifts too, not just receive them, so the Friendly One also takes something in return. At times it can be something very unfortunate, like your mouth.

All in all I think it's good that they don't notice me, especially as they're still learning how to be good friends.
# Forest Song
All mushroom circles are unique and in most of them I can play without fear. Even around my home there is a beautiful mushroom circle! But only few know that each of them sings their own quiet song.

There are circles so strong, they sing the ancient song of the forest and you can do nothing but listen in fascination.

I am not charmed by such songs, because my Mommy sings them to me, but sometimes I see how a fox or a deer falls asleep in a circle and then trees, grass and mushrooms reach with their roots towards them.

Everything happens rather quickly and only a flower covered mound is left behind. This is how animals become part of the song and the forest grows.

I think the same would happen to a human if they listened closely.
# The Unfair Healer
Helping others and being kind is very important, especially if you all live in a place where there may be a lot of danger. I always follow this rule and try to help others in the forest!

This is why once I ran after a rabbit who had problems with his footie. I wanted to help before a sly fox or a sharp eyed owl would catch him, but he kept losing me. In the end, we ran very deep into the woods where we stumbled across a small clay figurine of a pigeon.

The pigeon was beautiful and pleasant to the touch, feeling slightly warm. It would be a great toy, if not for its strange ability. When the rabbit touched the figurine running past it, my arm started hurting and looking wrong.

It hurt a lot, but the rabbit fleeing my sight now looked healthy.

At night Mommy explained that the figurine heals but at the cost of the health of others. Then she asked me to break my arm again so that it could heal properly.

I buried the figurine and did not leave a mark where I left it. I fixed my arm too. It's good that I always find the strength to do as my Mommy says when she asks me to.
# Cry of War
Very often, I find some peculiar objects in the forest which I immediately bring to my hiding spots. After all, no treasure should lie around just like that!

Sometimes when I'm very lucky I'm able to find something truly, ancient, like a tiny piece of an armor or a sword. Often they are very sturdy and one can take a peek at the red and gold colored metal that can be seen through the rust and moss.

Such armor and weapons are very responsive to the bare touch, so they give you a slight feeling of distant pain. Once, I felt it in my heart and another time around my neck. Many, many of these little pieces exist!

However, one of the pieces I own is very valuable. When touching it, you feel an unbearable amount of sadness. It's so strong that you immediately start crying. When I touched it for the first time it felt like if I could endure it a little bit, I'd be able to realise what caused me to feel this way... something very big and scary. But I didn't manage to understand why so much pain was contained in such a small piece, because a raven pecked it out of my hands in time.

Now when I find such treasures I wrap them in rags and put them in separate hiding spots so that people won't find them and ever experience this kind of pain at all.
# The Precious Owl
It's hard to tell what's going on between animals when you don't speak their language. Now it's not a problem anymore, but back then I could only watch their mysterious life.

Once I met an owl that kept very majestically in the hollow of an old and twisted tree.

The owl was just enormous, it could have easily grabbed and swallowed me! It was because of her huge size that she never left her home.

From time to time, mice and rats would run into the hollow with all sorts of trinkets: buttons, buckles, or other metal things. The owl didn't eat mice, but she was happy to swallow the treasures. The most interesting thing was that sometimes little mice brought droppings out of the hollow, shiny as gold coins, and then piled it up in all places well-known to the ravens.

The ravens were annoyed that these strange droppings shimmered so brightly in the light, but I think that was actually the intention of that very mean owl.

My mommy said that sometimes gold comes from unpleasant sources and only self-respecting birds can refuse the easy but insulting glitter.
# Staring Contest
Sometimes you can find a shard of a mirror in the woods, and sometimes it reflects a silhouette. This silhouette is curious, but it does nothing at all, just stares intently.

If you "catch" one by looking into the mirror, it starts watching you from all the things that can reflect!

But since there aren't that many shiny things in the woods, you don't always realize there are now many pairs of prying eyes following you around.

Once I felt very tired and scared for a couple of days, but since it was after the full moon, I blamed it all on the waning of the cycle. But then I went to the river for a drink and noticed that there were five of them staring back at me from the reflection!

The next day my mommy swiftly chased them away, but ever since I try to look into strange mirror shards as little as possible.
# The Nice Uncle
I rarely meet him at all especially because people are such rare guests in the forest and those searching for their loved ones who left for the woods are an even rarer sight. These are the people the Nice Uncle is usually appearing before.

He's just as tall as a young pine and very kind. Oh, and you can see his teeth as well! He doesn't have any eyes though and he actually looks like a really old corpse!

I feel like people don't recognize that his appearance is a *biiit* different from theirs, so they happily grasp his hand and follow him.

I think that the Nice Uncle promises to lead them to the person they were looking for, but to be honest it's a bit hard to understand what he's actually saying.

When he was showing the way to another girl once, I decided to follow him. At some point both of them disappeared somewhere, but later I actually found her under the tree, next to someone's skeleton.

Although she wasn't alive by that point, she still looked satisfied, which means that the Nice Uncle fulfilled his promise and sent her off

He's not reacting at all when he sees me, but my Mommy said it's because I'm not looking for anyone.
# Forest Mail
Before my friends came from the mists, I've never written any letters. It's different now and I'm sending mail to everyone we meet on our travels!

But even before that, I had a pen pal, even if only briefly.

One day I found a pretty jewelry box lying in some ruins. There was a key inside, but it didn't open anything, so I buried it somewhere else and put my twig doll in the jewelry box.

I forgot all about it after a while, until one day I decided to check on my doll. But when I opened it, I instead found a moldy piece of bread inside! Next I put three gold coins in the box, because I wouldn't be sorry to lose them, and on the next day they were gone and there was bread again!

This way I exchanged all kinds of stuff with some mysterious stranger, until one day I received a note saying: "I will find you"

I didn't want to put my new friend at risk, so I had to stop our exchange and hide the jewelry box back where I found it.

Anyway, it's good to know that, if necessary, one can use it to get in touch with someone kind and send them a note, letting them know where you are!
# The Bush of Memories
There are so many interesting and varied plants in my forest! Among them grows a bush my Mommy really dislikes. When it becomes so big it reaches my burrow, she immediately removes it.

But I'll explain to you what it does if you ever encounter it on your journey!

If you bury an animal next to it, then, after some time, the bush will grow berries. They have a very interesting form and look a bit similar to water drops or tears.

If you eat one, you will very vividly "remember" what happened to the buried animal.

My Mommy showed me how the berries can affect people. Some get curious to "remember" what it feels like to be a raven or a fox. But, after eating a few of those, it's very hard to stop eating them.

In the end they get trapped and feel so lost among those memories that it's hard for them to remember who they once were.

But guess what I'm really curious about! If you bury a person underneath this bush, will you be able to "remember" their memories as well?
# Stairs
I think there used to be a few very interesting buildings in the forest, especially in those parts that are closer to human settlements.

I don't know what happened, but all that's left of those old houses now are ruins where you can play to your heart's content!

But there are a couple of rules. For example, you can never ever go up any stairs that lead into nowhere.

A few times I saw how some not very smart little squirrels did exactly that. I really, really hope that they're in a better place now and that their parents don't miss them!
# The Knowledge
South of my burrow, next to the old deadwood, there is a small circle of stones spread around a charred, black tree stump.

I discovered it accidently many, many years before, but I've rarely visited this place at all.

I can't really say that there was anything particularly wrong with it besides the withering grass and a few little dead animals lying around the circle.

But the most interesting thing about it was that if you actually step into the circle and touch the stump with both of your hands you would suddenly get some kind of epiphany, just as sudden as if you were hit by the lightning! That's how I discovered the existence of chess and how to play them, even if I've never owned a chess board before! Another time after touching the tree stump I've even learned how to harvest millet!

After that I didn't feel good at all, though, and I've coughed up blood for some time. So I've never touched the tree stump again.

I think that it can give the person touching it some random knowledge, but I doubt it can be done more than three times.
# The Leshy
He's very very nice! But when you look at him for a long time, your eyes begin to hurt. The Leshy is very fluffy, with moss and small mushrooms growing on him.

He's always walking around the forest here and there, to see that everything's in order. The Leshy punishes those who hunt at the wrong time and disturb the woods.

You have to be very polite with him! Don't do anything naughty. He doesn't talk, but he bows when he meets you and is very well-mannered.

One time I gave him a flower to thank him for all his hard work keeping the forest in order! Afterwards pretty spring flowers were blooming around my burrow for the entire winter.
